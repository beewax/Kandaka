## Review method and corrections

Commons official imageinfo/extmetadata records were retrieved for 20 distinct sources. Eight originals were downloaded and visually inspected. Further original downloads were retried but remained unavailable. Capture dates were separated from upload, enhancement and book-publication dates. Semna is a detail photographed at the relocated museum temple, not its original river site. The Meroe chapel is not a panoramic view. NASA's 1973 agency date was not mistaken for capture: the image was taken on 6 May 1993. Faris was captured in July 2011, not uploaded in 2014. Gordon's Tree was published in 1906; exact capture date is unspecified.

Official Library of Congress JSON records yielded nine Matson photographs. All nine source JPEGs were downloaded and visually inspected. Catalogue date [1936] is approximate; nationality, religion, personal names, income and uncertain bird species were not inferred from appearance. The caretaker's catalogue role is attributed, without repeating a colonial generalisation. Rights remain 'No known restrictions on publication', not falsely relabelled public domain. The collection rights handle now redirects to a dynamic general guide; each item's explicit rights advisory is the evidence used, with direct item links.

Wellcome API verified four records: lithograph, two process prints, and a medical photograph reproduction. The latter remains held, not misclassified as a drawing. SMVK direct source verified James Hornell's museum-tools photograph but not a capture date or clear reuse terms (1939 is acquisition). Finna API and eSbírky records were inaccessible; no metadata or provenance invented. One LOC book record was inaccessible and remains explicitly unresolved.

English and Arabic describe the same images and limitations. Historical photos are not represented as present conditions. No AI reconstruction, article release, artwork edit or automation change was made.
