# Image rights and caption audit

Nine references audited, including the cover. None of the article’s nine actual files was supplied. Published source plates and candidate Commons file pages were checked; this is not certification of pixel identity, dimensions, crops or edits in the publication assets. Every image is held inactive in the revised article until an actual asset is matched. A hold records missing evidence; it does not claim infringement or require a new commission.

Historic copyright status, scan provenance and later creative transformations are separate questions. The 1910 works are out of U.S. copyright; modern plans and commissioned art require their own evidence. Commons licensing is recorded as observed on 19 September 2026.
