# Claim-by-claim verification

108 stable claim records. Research cut-off: 2026-09-19. Exact excerpts are taken from the supplied draft after decoding HTML entities; punctuation and wording are retained. Blocks are zero-based blank-line blocks in the preserved original. Repeated claims are cross-covered; image rights repeated in five captions have separate records.

Verdicts: **supported** means the cited evidence supports the claim; **supported with qualification** means a material limit must accompany it; **contradicted** means evidence or arithmetic conflicts with the wording; **uncertain** means a live interpretive issue; **unverified** means the necessary evidence was not supplied or inspected. For rhetorical groups, “supported with qualification” means compatible with the evidence as an argument, not objectively proved. Blank citations for commissioning claims mean no records were supplied, not proof that a commission never existed.
