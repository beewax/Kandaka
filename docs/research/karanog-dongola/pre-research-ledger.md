## Core research sources

- C. Leonard Woolley and David Randall-MacIver, *Karanog: The Romano-Nubian Cemetery*, vols. III–IV (University Museum, 1910). Public-domain excavation record; the user-supplied scan was verified as volume III, the text volume. It supports the grave count, excavation chronology, findspots, division of objects and the excavators' admission that some superstructures were destroyed to reach chambers. The article treats its chronology, “Romano-Nubian” terminology, “Blemyan” identification and racial typology as historical interpretations rather than current conclusions. Digitised text: https://archive.org/details/karangromanonu00wool; digitised plates: https://mc.dlib.nyu.edu/files/books/ifa_egypt000402/ifa_egypt000402_lo.pdf
- Bruce B. Williams, *Meroitic Remains from Qustul Cemetery Q, Ballana Cemetery B, and a Ballana Settlement* (Oriental Institute Nubian Expedition 8, 1991), especially the Lower Nubian relative chronology. https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/oine8pt1.pdf
- László Török, *The Kingdom of Kush: Handbook of the Napatan-Meroitic Civilization* (Brill, 1997), used for the modern critique of the old “Romano-Nubian” framing and Karanog's importance to Meroitic Lower Nubia.
- Penn Museum Egyptian Section and Karanog expedition archive. https://www.penn.museum/about-collections/curatorial-sections/egyptian-section and https://collections.penn.museum/collections/archives/findingaid/552795
- Włodzimierz Godlewski, *Dongola—Ancient Tungul: Archaeological Guide* (PCMA, 2013), especially the Throne Hall and Kingdom of Dongola chapters. https://pcma.uw.edu.pl/2018/01/04/dongola/
- Polish Centre of Mediterranean Archaeology, University of Warsaw, Dongola mission page and 2012 conservation report. https://pcma.uw.edu.pl/en/2019/04/02/dongola-2/ and https://pcma.uw.edu.pl/badania/sezon-po-sezonie/dongola-sudan-5/
