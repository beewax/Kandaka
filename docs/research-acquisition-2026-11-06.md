Quick win restricted inventories; five-year local custody/skills networks; long-term repaired museums. Cheaper option metadata-first inventory; risk theft exposure and sustainability of IT. - **Displaced crafts and living heritage (capacity building, Oct 2026):** 25 trainees from Khartoum's seven localities; not yet measured safeguarding results. Quick win community-consent interviews and bilingual metadata; five-year locally stewarded practices and training. Cost unknown; cheaper option licensed links rather than media duplication; risk harm, loss of consent and community appropriation.
- **Kenya/Tanzania ruNbS (implemented 2019–25; 2026 case study):** seven green-grey flood works; reportedly ~5% in-kind community contributions, 66 monitoring indicators, no comparable capital cost. Quick win drain inventories/clearance and shaded safe passages; five-year neighborhood drains coordinated with municipal engineers; long-term floodplain protection. Cheaper alternative existing drain repair; risks tenure, sanitation, upkeep and false causal attribution.
