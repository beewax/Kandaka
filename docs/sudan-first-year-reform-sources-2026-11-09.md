## Sudan specific evidence

**Eltahir, Yassin; Ahmed Abdelmotlab; ElSiddig Yousif Mohammed Mousa; Fuad Yousif (December 2021).** *The Sudan economy “challenge and suggestions” Meta study*. Global Journal of Economics and Business 11(3), 471–476; DOI [10.31559/GJEB2021.11.3.11](https://doi.org/10.31559/GJEB2021.11.3.11). [Publisher PDF](https://www.refaad.com/Files/GJEB/GJEB-11-3-11.pdf), seven PDF pages including cover; printed 471–476 reviewed. Four pre-war studies combined using author-assigned scores; high heterogeneity and nonsignificant random-effects result limit inference. Recommendations include support-system preparation before subsidy withdrawal. No causal policy-effect estimate used. PDF states CC BY 4.0. Already in Kandaka; Gmail discovery verified privately. RSR-173.

**Elbadawi, Ibrahim; Abdelrazig Elbashir; Abdelrahman Osman; Amir Hamid Elobaid; Elfatih Eltahir; Alzaki Alhelo; Kabbashi M. Suliman (July 2022).** *Sudan’s Challenges and Opportunities: A Renaissance Project for Sudan: From Poor Agriculture to Agro-Industrial Growth and Sustainable Development*. ERF Policy Research Report 40. [Original PDF](https://erf.org.eg/app/uploads/2022/07/1659257696_282_6382995_prr40.pdf). Central calibre-2776 EPUB; selected introduction/policy passages reviewed, not full report. Proposes agricultural productivity and institutional change; not an evaluated post-war programme. Publisher copyright retained; FCDO funding disclosed. Distinct from November 2022 PB 99 with four listed authors. No PDF mirroring. RSR-152.

**World Bank (October 2026).** *Macro Poverty Outlook: Sudan*, two pages, [official PDF](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/bae48ff2fefc5a869546775b3f010735-0500062021/related/mpo-sdn.pdf); [dated record](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099647210162618742). Text reviewed; estimates/projections depend on conflict assumptions and limited data. Does not price a new government's programme. Broader institutional country pages and AfDB forecasts differ; avoid mixing vintages or poverty definitions. No graph reproduced or new hosting. RSR-084.

**Ali, Muez; Muzan Alneel; Mayada Hassanain (23 January 2025 online).** *Conditionality Breeds Contempt: Donor and Multilateral Myopia in Sudan*, African Studies Review. [Publisher article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/african-studies-review/article/conditionality-breeds-contempt-donor-and-multilateral-myopia-in-sudan/4B4617CA4344272104F80E5C50D44362). Selected political-economy argument reviewed, not an impact evaluation. Warns that reform design neglected political power and public participation. Competing interpretations require examination; not proof that economic reform caused the coup or war. Publisher states CC BY 4.0. RSR-176.

**World Bank (29 July 2022).** *Frequently Asked Questions on the Recently Approved Emergency Safety Nets Project in Sudan*. [Original account](https://www.worldbank.org/en/brief/2022/07/29/frequently-asked-questions-on-the-recently-approved-emergency-safety-nets-project-in-sudan). Relevant programme/funding sections read: Thamarat operations paused after October 2021, with exceptional later support delivered through WFP. Historical implementation and funding continuity matter; this source is not a 2026 operational coverage audit. RSR-084.

[Repository](https://media.suub.uni-bremen.de/entities/publication/a599b0b6-6164-47d4-bf6b-ae3471351a3c), DOI 10.26092/elib/3067. ## Comparative reviews

**Balima, Hippolyte W.; Anna Sokolova (2021).** *IMF programs and economic growth: A meta-analysis*, Journal of Development Economics 153, 102741. DOI [10.1016/j.jdeveco.2021.102741](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdeveco.2021.102741). Final publisher abstract/sections indexed; direct page 403. Selected methods/conclusions/publication-bias passages read in the separate [17 September 2020 HSE working paper](https://wp.hse.ru/data/2021/01/12/1346499911/241EC2020.pdf), WP BRP 241/EC/2020. 994 estimates from 36 studies, heterogeneous positive mean; estimates are not independent experiments. Versions kept distinct. Full final article not claimed read. No redistribution permission established. RSR-174.

**Fernández Salguero, Ricardo Alonzo (5 July 2026).** *IMF Programs and Growth: A Source-Informed Robustness Reanalysis*, arXiv:2607.04468v1. [Full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.04468v1). Methods, interpretation and limitations reviewed; preprint, no peer-reviewed status established. Questions robustness under study dependence and credibility assumptions; neither proves exact zero nor universal harm. Replication not rerun; credibility coding and small subgroups remain limitations. CC BY 4.0 displayed. RSR-175.

**Thomson, Michael; Alexander Kentikelenis; Thomas Stubbs (10 July 2017).** *Structural adjustment programmes adversely affect vulnerable populations: a systematic-narrative review of their effect on child and maternal health*, Public Health Reviews 38, 13. DOI [10.1186/s40985-017-0059-2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40985-017-0059-2). Abstract, methods and conclusion reviewed; 13 studies, predominantly adverse associations, observational/heterogeneous evidence. A systematic narrative review, not a pooled effect size for Sudan. No quantified health effect used. Open access; exact reuse terms to check before any mirroring. RSR-178.

**IMF (30 June 2021).** *Sudan: Request for a 39-Month Arrangement Under the Extended Credit Facility*, Country Report 21/142. [Record](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/cr/issues/2021/06/30/sudan-request-for-a-39-month-arrangement-under-the-extended-credit-facility-press-release-461358). Selected programme objectives and social-support/monetary framework passages indexed/read; not full report. A historical proposed programme, not evidence that all commitments were implemented or a currently available facility. RSR-085.
