# Sudanese hair history — commissioned research brief

21 November 2026. Owner review required before publication. This is a research brief, not a finished article.

## Proposed feature

Braids, Barbers and Belonging: A History of Hair in Sudan
الضفائر والحلاقة والانتماء: تاريخ الشعر وتسريحاته في السودان

One substantial illustrated bilingual feature, with balanced women's and men's coverage; consider splitting only if the evidence and reading length justify it. Target approximately 3,000–4,000 English words with full Arabic counterpart, not a literal word-count target. Accessible historical storytelling grounded in specific people, tools, objects and dated pictures.

## Article architecture

1. Start with the skilled work of arranging hair; an attributed practitioner account rather than an invented salon scene.
2. What ancient evidence can show: ornaments, combs, preserved hair and depictions; distinguish natural hair, wigs and headgear. Ancient Nubia crosses today's national border. No automatic descent claim for modern mushat.
3. Medieval and later images: use verified examples where hair is visible; acknowledge documentary gaps rather than constructing an uninterrupted timeline.
4. Women's braiding: mushat terminology, regional variations, named styles, occasions and the mushata's expertise. Verify spellings/meanings with Sudanese practitioners; no universal marital/status code.
5. Men's hair: substantial section on dated regional examples, including eastern Sudan, then barbering, urban styles and changing fashions. Avoid colonial caricatures and ethnic essentialism.
6. Tools and materials: khulal, combs, ornaments and products, with object-specific provenance and use; no unsupported medical benefits.
7. Twentieth-century change and today's salons/diaspora: dated photographs, oral testimony and Sudanese creators, not a claim that one generation abandoned tradition.
8. Illustrated glossary/timeline: only sufficiently evidenced styles; dates describe the record, not necessarily origin. Conclude with living creativity and skill.

## العربية — نطاق العمل

إعداد مقال موسّع ومصوّر عن تاريخ المشاط وتسريحات الرجال في السودان، يوازن بين آثار النوبة والصور التاريخية وشهادات الممارسين. يُبحث تنوّع الأساليب بين المناطق والفترات، ودور المشّاطة والحلّاق وأدوات الشعر والزينة وتغيّر الذوق. لا تُختزل تسريحات الرجال في فقرة جانبية، ولا تُعرض التقاليد وكأنها ثابتة منذ العصور القديمة. يلزم التحقّق من أسماء التسريحات ومعانيها ومواقع الصور وتواريخها وحقوق استخدامها. تُعرض النسختان العربية والإنجليزية والصور المقترحة على صاحب الموقع قبل النشر. هذه خطة بحث موثّقة وليست مسودة نهائية أو إذناً بالنشر.
