# Kandaka research scope addition — Art, Archaeology, Urban Planning

**Status:** RESEARCH REQUIREMENT APPROVED; no article or Library publication authorized.
**Canonical editorial record:** KANDAKA_MASTER_WORKPLAN.md. Integrate this addendum at the next master-workplan update; do not replace newer article statuses.

## Standing research coverage
- **Art and cultural industries:** Sudanese visual arts, photography, film, textiles/crafts, museums, design, creative-economy training, conservation, exhibition and community ownership. Search Sudanese institutions and UNESCO/ICOM, museum collections, arts archives, scholarly journals and regional comparators.
- **Archaeology and heritage:** Nubia, Kush, Funj and later history; Sudanese-led excavation, heritage documentation, endangered sites, community archaeology, digital preservation, museum practice, cultural tourism and site protection. Seek excavation reports, primary inventories, Sudanese scholarship, UNESCO/ICCROM/ICOMOS and archaeological mission archives. Do not overclaim provenance or historical continuity.
- **Urban planning:** affordable climate-adapted housing, 5–10-minute access to essential services, transit and dedicated lanes, shade, heat, flood drainage, dust, safe walking/cycling, disability/elder access, land rights, municipal governance, locally produced construction and transport components. Search Sudanese universities, ministries, UN-Habitat and implemented case studies.

## International examples and implementation test
Search Africa, Asia, Middle East and Latin America as well as Europe/US for proven practical interventions, not just aspirational plans. Prioritize low-cost, decentralized, locally manufactured/maintained solutions. Each candidate must record: project/location and original source; verified results and limitations; cost and recurring maintenance where available; implementing institution; skills/materials; suitability for Sudanese heat/flood/dust/security; accessibility and marginalized-community benefit; opportunity cost; cheaper alternatives; adaptation steps; and tier (quick win / five-year / long-term / not yet).
