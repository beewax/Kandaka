### A13

Sudan-specific contemporary editorial scene inspired by the built environment of Omdurman Souq. A working grain merchant lane with single-storey red brick and sun-faded plaster shopfronts, green metal shutters, corrugated sheet awnings, grain sacks and metal weighing scales, a modest motorised cargo tricycle. Sudanese adults in a natural mix of roomy white Sudanese jalabiyas with small round white caps, everyday shirts and trousers; a woman customer wears a Sudanese tobe, a single long cloth loosely wrapped around body and head. Specific urban Nile Sudan, not an exotic village or fantasy bazaar. Natural muted sunlight, realistic contemporary editorial illustration, fine detail, no orange-and-teal filter, no romantic poverty scene. Fictional people and business, no real identifiable individuals, no claim of current reportage. Wide 16:9 composition at street eye level, safe margins. No thatched huts, domes, minarets, pyramids, desert caravans, jewellery stereotypes, decorative motifs, flags, readable signs, brands or watermark.

### A15

Sudan, Gezira agricultural plain, contemporary editorial illustration grounded in photographs of the actual flat irrigated landscape. Wide eye-level view across dark grey-brown clay soil, a narrow earthen gravity irrigation channel crossing rectangular small fields, low green sorghum rows, immense level horizon and pale cloudy sky. Two adult Sudanese farmers checking the channel in the middle distance: one wears a loose off-white Sudanese jalabiya and small white round cap, the other a faded blue work shirt and dark trousers, carrying long wooden-handled hoes. Natural unposed activity, modest human scale, no portrait closeup. Very realistic restrained painted illustration, natural colours, no orange filter, no fantasy village. Fictional scene informed by Gezira photographs, not documentary evidence or a promise of harvest. Landscape 16:9, uncluttered, strong horizontal composition. No mountains, dunes, thatched huts, red-roof houses, pyramids, domes, minarets, flags, aid logos, writing or watermark.
