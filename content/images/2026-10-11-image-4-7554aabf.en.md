The Náprstek Museum catalogue dates this photograph to 1927 and attributes its location to Kordofan, Sudan. The photographer and the subjects’ names are unrecorded. Its historical labels “Nubové” and “Bagirmi” have not been independently resolved and are not treated here as verified community identities or a precise location. Holding these objects does not establish the man's occupation or participation in combat.
