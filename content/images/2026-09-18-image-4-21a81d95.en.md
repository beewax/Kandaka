A two-handled Meroitic amphora in the Finnish Heritage Agency collection (KM17000:39:116:2), catalogued broadly to 300 BCE–400 CE. The record associates it with Nubia but lists both Egypt and Sudan, so no precise modern-country findspot is asserted here. The maker and photographic date are unrecorded. This is a museum object photograph, not a view of present-day Sudan.
