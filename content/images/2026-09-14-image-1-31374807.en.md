A view from Jebel Barkal near Karima, Sudan, photographed by Maurice Chédel on 14 March 1984. Archaeological remains lie below the mountain, with buildings, cultivated land and the Nile beyond. The source identifies the Amun temple at the mountain’s foot. This is a scan of a historical slide, reproduced without further alteration; it does not show present conditions.
