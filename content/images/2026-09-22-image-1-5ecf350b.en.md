The carved entrance of the Hathor chapel at Naqa, Sudan, photographed by Nina R on 26 March 2016. The columns, arched openings and winged sun-disc decoration match the chapel documented by the Naga excavation team. The source file's title and description incorrectly refer to pyramid reliefs at Meroe; Kandaka has corrected that identification. This photograph records the monument in 2016, not its present condition.

[Photograph and source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sudan,_Nubian_pyramids_(31443149591).jpg) · [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)
