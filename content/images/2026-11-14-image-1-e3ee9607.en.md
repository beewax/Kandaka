A photographed scale model of ancient Kerma on display in the Kerma Museum in Sudan. The model shows the Deffufa and surrounding buildings, lanes and enclosure walls. Matthias Gehricke took this photograph on 27 December 2018, as recorded in the source and camera metadata. The model presents an interpretation of the ancient city. This dated photograph gives no assurance about current displays or visitor access.
